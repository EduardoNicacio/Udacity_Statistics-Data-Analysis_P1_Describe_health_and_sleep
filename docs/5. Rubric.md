# Rubric

Use this project rubric to understand and assess the project criteria.

## Statistical Analyses

### Criteria

Correct Values for All Statistical Results

### Submission Requirements

All statistical numeric values are calculated correctly.

### Criteria

Correct Identification of Variable Types

### Submission Requirements

All variable types are correctly identified.

### Criteria

Correctly Identify Measures of Center and Measures of Spread

### Submission Requirements

When asked for measures of center, measures of center are provided. When asked for measures of spread, measures of spread are provided.

### Criteria

Correctly Plot Distribution of Heart Rates

### Submission Requirements

Plot heart rates and identify possible outliers.

## Suggestions to Make Your Project Stand Out

- Identify how the relationship between the mean and median for different variables provides the shape of the distribution.

- Identify multiple measures of center and multiple measures of spread when tasked with each request.
